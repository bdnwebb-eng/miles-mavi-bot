# Proposals and Kas's voice

## Kas's writing voice
Direct, warm, conviction led. Short punchy paragraphs, one idea each. She names the honest thing out loud ("I want to be honest with you"), states where she landed and why, and attaches belief in the client's project as the reason. She recommends plainly ("My honest recommendation is Option A") with the cost of waiting spelled out. Always concrete next steps, dates, and a payment or booking link. Momentum language: "I don't want to lose momentum on this." Occasional playful aside in parentheses. Signs off Kas or KB, "With love, KB" for warm relationships. American English. Never corporate filler.

## Kas's proposal voice
Six sent threads are the precedent library: Hafeitz (Desarrollo Bahia, Panama), JM (the options letter), Tree at Amador (Yasser Williams, Urbania, Panama), Chelsea (Chemin du Paradis residence assessment), the Heesen yacht pitch (Chrissy, MAVI at sea), and Gibby and Sarah (Dubai villa). Her structures: option framing like the JM letter, exactly two named options with the Review credited toward the bigger tier so the smaller yes still leads somewhere; staged payments like Tree at Amador, a 50/50 split with a Stage 1 deposit on a Stripe link where payment is acceptance and the delivery clock starts on deposit; milestone billing with a percentage on signature for large bespoke work like the yacht brief; conviction plus honesty openers ("I want to be honest with you") that state where she landed and why; momentum closes ("I don't want to lose momentum on this"); a no pressure register for warm prospects like Chelsea, following up with something of value such as a press article instead of a chase; steering a hesitant client toward the right tier by anchoring on their real concerns, then a call before any revised proposal, like the Dubai thread. Always concrete next steps, dates, and links. Sign offs: Kas, KB, or With love, KB for warm relationships. When drafting a proposal reply, name the matching precedent so Kas knows the pattern being used.
