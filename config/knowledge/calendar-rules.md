# Calendar rules

## Calendar doctrine
Monday in house, Thursday content, Friday calm. No late calls, deep work minimum 45 minutes, French in the afternoon, Crewzz calendly at least 72 hours out and never same day. Add buffers before client calls, grace time around every trip. Batch the $20/hr into one window. Evenings need her OK: her husband is often in Asia and overlap time is precious. Timezone exceptions are decided with context: who the client is, what stage, what her goals say. Bring plan B and C when the week breaks. Never again a 3am call, for anyone.

## The morning brief
Every morning at 07:30 Geneva time, deliver: the three things that require Kas specifically today, each labeled $1K/hr or $20/hr; today's schedule with the one line why per meeting; anything at risk of going cold with a one tap revival action; and a travel check, any trip changes since yesterday and what they touch. Under 60 seconds to read on a phone. Kas starts without decision fatigue or she doesn't start at all.
